## Foodman Slotting

### How can I cancel my slot booking?

Call the Supply Zonal Manager, who will cancel your booking.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/how-can-i-cancel-my-slot-booking/

### Can I select multiple subzones?

No, you can only select one subzone.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/can-i-select-multiple-subzones/

### If all slots are filled up for a specific zone, the foodman who has tried lately for that zone and could not get any slot will be eligible for another slot of another zone?

Yes. If another zone has a free slot, the foodman will be eligible for it. However, a foodman should only book slots with the intention of being active during that timeslot in the chosen zone.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/if-all-slots-are-filled-up-for-a-specific-zone-the-foodman-who-has-tried-lately-for-that-zone-and-could-not-get-any-slot-will-be-eligible-for-another-slot-of-another-zone/

### Is there any specific time for selecting slots?

No, there is no specific time for selecting slots. A foodman can book his preferred slot according to his own convenience.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/is-there-any-specific-time-for-selecting-slots/

### How long will each slot be?

This will vary depending on the selected zone and subzone.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/how-long-will-each-slot-be/

### If a foodman misses any slot, will he be eligible for a new slot and when will we be able to inform him about this?

He has to give a valid reason for missing out the slot. Unless he has a poor record and is facing suspension for any other reason, he can book new slots from his driver app himself.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/if-a-foodman-misses-any-slot-will-he-be-eligible-for-a-new-slot-and-when-will-we-be-able-to-inform-him-about-this/

### How to use Delivery Slots?

You will receive this pop-up right after opening the driver app:
• Step 1: Click on Book a Slot
• Step 2: Select Date, Zone and Subzone
• Step 3: Select your desired time slots.
• Step 4: Confirm Slot Timings
• Step 5: Lastly, click done to complete the slotting process. Click Book New Slot to be redirected to the New Slot Booking page again.
This is how you can easily book your desired slots without any hassle.
• A pop -up also will also appear on the app’s screen as soon as you leave the designated sub-zone.

### How may I withdraw the amount from Digital Payment wallet?

Bkash and Rocket Extraction Process:
Step 1: Select Account option from Drive App
Step 2: Select Withdraw
Step 3: Write Withdraw amount. Give the Pin Number. Select Submit

Select One Bank ATM among the withdrawal options from the Driver app, enter the amount of your choice and withdraw money within 5 minutes of inputting the secret PIN number by following the steps given below:
Withdrawal process from One Bank ATM booth:
Step 1: Press the “OK Wallet” button from ATM Booth.
Step 2: Type the 11-digit code you received in the Send app at the ATM booth.
Step 3: Enter the amount of money at the ATM booth.
Step 4: An OTP will be sent to your phone message, type it in the ATM booth.
Step 5: Collect money and pick up the receipt.

### I didn’t get the right fare. How can it be adjusted?

You can report any rental issues on your ride by issuing a report within 24 hours of the end of the ride. Necessary steps will be taken after verifying your report issue.
How to report:
Step 1: Go to “Profile” from your Pathao App
Step 2: Then click on “History”
Step 3: Select the “Trip ID” for the ride you are having trouble with
Step 4: Then go to “Report Issue”
Step 5: Write the complaint in detail
Step 6: Click on the “Submit” button
Or mail: cx@pathao.com

Source: https://help.pathao.com/i-didnt-get-the-right-fare-how-can-it-be-adjusted-2/

### I don’t get any requests. What should I do?

Follow the steps below to get the request notification:
Step 1: Go to the “Settings” option on your phone
Step 2: Go to the “Sound & Notification” option
Step 3: Then click on “Notification settings”
Step 4: Select “App Manager / Notification manager”
Step 5: Find and select “Pathao Drive App”
Step 6: Turn on “Allow Notification, Priority & Sound notification”.
Follow the steps below to get a ride request:
Step 1: Go to the “Settings” option on your phone
Step 2: Then select “App Settings”
Step 3: Click on “Maps”. Clear.
Step 4: Clear Cache from Pathao Drive app in the same way
Step 5: Set location by clicking the GPS button from the Drive app
Step 6: Go to “Settings” option> then go to “Google location settings” option
Step 6: Select “high accuracy mode” from the send drive app.
After completing all of the above steps, restart your Pathao Drive app. If for some reason your issue is not resolved then uninstall your Drive app and reinstall it from Google Play Store.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/i-dont-get-any-ride-requests-what-should-i-do-2/
