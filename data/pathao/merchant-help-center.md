## New Merchant

### Can I work with Pathao by integrating Pathao’s panel with my website?

Sure, you can integrate your website’s API with Pathao’s Merchant panel. You can do this from the Developer API option in Pathao’s merchant panel.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/integrate-pathao-panel-with-website/

### Pickup requests must be confirmed by how many hours per day?

For same day delivery panel entry should be done by selecting express delivery before 12 noon and panel entry should be confirmed before 4 pm for standard delivery.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/pickup-requests-confirmation-hours/

### I don’t have a Facebook page/website, can I register as a merchant of Pathao?

Facebook page/website is not mandatory to join as a merchant.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/no-facebook-or-website-merchant-registration-pathao/

### How can I track my order?

Go to “Deliveries” option from the panel. From there click on “All” option and you can view and check the details of the parcel you want to check.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/track-order/

### What to do if a product is lost or damaged?

Please order information and With before and after pictures of damage The panel can issue a report on that order in the following ways-
> Click on the product you are facing the problem from the Deliveries option of the Dashboard panel
> Select the issue type by clicking on Report Issue option from More, enter details and attach image and submit.Or email us at ecomsupport@pathao.com with order information and before and after photos of damage from your registered email.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/product-lost-damaged/

### How can I issue reports?

> Please go to the Deliveries option from merchant panel.
> View the Consignment Id that encountered the problem.
> Go to More option and click Report Issue from the dropdown.
> Then a popup window will appear, select problem type, enter details and attach image (if any) and submit.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/how-can-i-issue-reports/

### How and for how long can I hold my product?

We can hold the product for a maximum of 3 days as there is a chance of damage to the product. If a hold is required for more than 3 days, please issue a report in the following ways:
> If an Account Manager (CAM) has been assigned for you, you (CAM) should inform the matter.
> Click on the product that you are facing the problem from the Deliveries option of the Panel Dashboard > Click on the More > Report Issue option. Select the type of problem there, enter the description and attach the image (if any) and submit. If possible, we will try to hold.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/how-long-can-i-hold-my-product/

### How do I return a product from a customer / make an entry for Reverse Pickup?

If a parcel delivered by Pathao Courier needs to be returned, view the delivered consignment order in the Deliveries option and select More > Reverse Delivery.
> If the previous order was not delivered by Pathao Courier, then create a normal delivery. Where write in your customer details provide your store address write “Reverse Pickup” in the special instruction box and The pickup address Enter the details and submit. In this case you have to contact the Key Account Manager (KAM) / Support before 4 PM.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/return-product-or-reverse-pickup-entry/

### How to change payment methods?

Follow the below mentioned steps”
Login to Merchant Panel
> Click on profile icon
>Go to Payment Information option.
> After confirming the password, a page will appear to update the payment method.
> For the bank, enter the account holder’s name and account number here and select the bank and branch from the dropdown list.
> If you want to add bKash number, enter bKash number and save.
> Within 24 hours, your new account will be approved through verification.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/how-to-change-payment-methods/

### Through which method and how will I receive my payment?

Currently we accept payments 5 days a week (all working days) through all banks and bkash.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/how-will-i-receive-payment/

### How can I make an entry for a partial order?

With the Partial Delivery option, you can request a new delivery by providing partial information. For partial delivery please go to dashboard <> New Deliveries <> Partial option. Click on the Partial button and a new page will appear. Save here with the following information.
> Store
> Customer’s phone number.
> Cash Collection Amount.
Note: If you have made a partial entry, you must complete the entry by 9 PM.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/make-an-entry-for-a-partial-order/

### How can I place bulk order / multiple orders at once?

Follow the steps below to enter the bulk order:
First Click on New Delivery > Bulk Order option. Then click on “Download Sample CSV” file, fill the information according to the column and save. Finally go to “Import CSV” option and confirm by uploading your filled excel sheet.
If there is a problem, it will be marked in red on the sheet. Which can be fixed and re-uploaded. Or you can check from “Go to error” option to see where the problem occurred.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/place-bulk-order-or-multiple-orders/

### How to create same day delivery?

Please, you will select the New Delivery option of your panel. Select “Same Day Delivery” as Delivery Type. Then provide the customer’s name, address, phone number and specify how much money you want to collect through cash on delivery in the cash amount option.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/create-same-day-delivery/

### How can I change my panel password?

You can easily change the panel password. Follow the steps below to change the password-
Go to > Profile>Password option and enter old password and new password and click on save option
> If you forget the password, you can reset the password via email from the reset password option from the merchant panel.
> If still facing any kind of problem, then contact our merchant support number from your registered number. The number is – 09610003030 (from 9 am to 11 pm call this number and press 9) or email from your registered email ecomsupport@pathao.com. Passwords will be reset by us.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/change-panel-password/

### What can I do if I have trouble logging into the merchant panel?

Please clear your browser cache and cookies and login again and then refresh by pressing Ctrl+Shift+R.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/trouble-logging-merchant-panel/

### How can I request for the store?

Please go to the Stores option in the panel. And click on the Create button and provide the following information.
> Store name.
> Store contact name.
> Contact phone number.
> Select City, Zone, Area.
> Detailed address of the store.
Note that from the panel you can create any number of stores as you require.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/request-for-the-store/

### Are there any weight or size restrictions for single or bulk parcels?

Currently, we do not have any weight or size restrictions for single or bulk parcels. But the parcel must be deliverable for e-commerce logistics.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/weight-or-size-restrictions-for-parcels/

### How do I create an order in the panel?

To add a new parcel, you can go to Dashboard > New Delivery in your panel. Then provide the customer’s name, address, phone number, specify the weight of the parcel, quantity and how much money you want to collect through cash on delivery in the cash amount option. Also, if there is any instruction, write it in the special instruction box.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/create-an-order-in-the-panel/

### What kind of service do you provide?

We currently have 3 types of courier services.
(i) You can use our merchant courier service if you are registered as a merchant.
(ii) Besides, even if you are not our merchant, you can take the customer-to-customer courier service by booking the parcel from our booking point.
(ii) If you want corporate delivery, you can contact our support number. The number is 09610003030 (from 9 am to 11 pm call this number and press 9).

Source: https://help.pathao.com/types-of-services/

## Pickup

### Does Pathao pick up parcels from merchants outside Dhaka?

Yes, I pick up parcels from 64 districts of Bangladesh.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/pathao-pickup-outside-dhaka/

### How to solve problems related to pick up?

Please follow the instructions below to avoid any problem related to pickup.
> Must collect a pickup slip from the pickup agent.
> Write merchant name, consignment ID, customer name, phone number and detailed address on the product. (possible If yes, take pictures of the product with and without packaging, weigh the product on the map device and keep pictures with the weight)
> Entries must be made within the stipulated time (last entry time is 4 pm)
Note: Please do not use a notepad on the parcel and write the required information on the parcel with a permanent marker.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/solve-pick-up-problems/

### When is your last pick up time?

Last time for normal delivery entry is 4 pm. Pickup starts from 2 PM and continues till 9 PM. Pickup for merchants outside Dhaka is open till 7 pm.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/last-pick-up-time/

### What is our delivery schedule?

Our delivery service is functional from 10 am to 7 pm and pickup service is from 2 pm to 9 pm.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/delivery-schedule/

### How can I get details about product check and return policy?

Please inform the customer to check the product in front of the delivery agent. If any problem is found in the product then take a picture of the product and give the product to the delivery agent immediately. If the customer receives the product and reports the problem afterwards, we have no opportunity to investigate the matter. In this case, we do not have the opportunity to take any kind of responsibility.
However, if the parcel is delivered through close box delivery, the customer does not get a chance to check the parcel. However if circumstances require checking the parcel, the delivery agent will contact the merchant and provide the checked return facility with the merchant’s permission.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/product-check-and-return-policy/

### Can the product be partially delivered in Pathao?

Yes, partial delivery can be done through Pathao. When the parcel is delivered to the customer, if the customer wants to take partial delivery from more than one parcel, our delivery agent will call you and inform you about it.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/product-partial-delivery/

### Can product exchange be delivered in Pathao?

Yes, exchange delivery can be done through Pathao. You can create an Exchange Parcel ID by going to the view option in the Delivered ID. Create an exchange ID and pick up the exchange parcel along with the regular parcel, our delivery agent will pick up the exchange parcel during delivery and you must inform the customer to return the return parcel to the delivery agent.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/can-product-exchange-be-delivered/
