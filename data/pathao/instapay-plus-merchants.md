## InstaPay Plus Payment Guide for Merchants

### Where can I find the On-Demand withdrawal option?

A dedicated withdrawal section will appear in your Courier Merchant Portal dashboard, showing your total withdrawable balance.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/where-can-i-find-the-on-demand-withdrawal-option/

### Who can use InstaPay On-Demand?

Only merchants added to the eligible On-Demand cohort will get access. If you are added, you will see the On-Demand withdrawal option in your Courier Merchant Portal.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/who-can-use-instapay-on-demand/

### How is On-Demand different from regular InstaPay?

• Regular InstaPay → Sends daily payouts (3 cycles) automatically.
• On-Demand → You manually withdraw only when you need money.If you use On-Demand, regular InstaPay will automatically be turned off.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/how-is-on-demand-different-from-regular-instapay/

### What is InstaPay On-Demand?

InstaPay On-Demand is a flexible withdrawal feature that lets courier merchants withdraw their COD money anytime they need. It does not require enabling regular InstaPay. Merchants continue using their normal payout method but can instantly transfer funds to their Pathao Pay Merchant Account whenever needed.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/what-is-instapay-on-demand/

### When You May Not Be Able to Withdraw

InstaPay Plus withdrawal may be restricted if:
• You have reached your maximum outstanding limit
• Your account does not meet eligibility criteria
• System risk controls temporarily restrict access

Source: https://help.pathao.com/7-when-you-may-not-be-able-to-withdraw/

### Important Things to Know

• InstaPay Plus eligibility and limits are updated daily
• You must withdraw the full approved amount (no partial withdrawals)
• Available InstaPay Plus may change throughout the day as order statuses update
• This feature is available only to eligible merchants

Source: https://help.pathao.com/6-important-things-to-know/

### How Repayment Works

InstaPay Plus repayment is automated and seamless.
• When an order is successfully delivered → the InstaPay Plus is adjusted from the collected COD
• If an order is returned or failed → the corresponding amount becomes due
• Any outstanding due is recovered from future successful deliveries or can be manually paid in the due option in the merchant dashboard.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/5-how-repayment-works/

### Fees & Charges

A service fee is applied to each InstaPay Plus disbursement.
The exact fee amount will be clearly shown before confirmation, and the merchant will receive the amount after the service charge has been deducted.
No hidden charges apply

Source: https://help.pathao.com/4-fees-charges/

### How to Withdraw InstaPay Plus?

Withdrawing your InstaPay Plus is simple:
• Go to your dashboard
• Click on the “Get Payment” button on the InstaPay Plus widget.
• Confirm the transaction
Once confirmed, the full approved amount will be instantly transferred to your Pathao Pay Merchant Wallet.
Note: Partial withdrawals are not supported.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/3-how-to-withdraw-instapay-plus/

### How is the InstaPay Plus Amount Calculated?

Your eligible InstaPay Plus amount is calculated daily based on your picked-up consignments.
The system considers:
• Total value of picked-up COD orders
• Predefined InstaPay Plus percentage
• Merchant eligibility, limits, and risk rules
The amount displayed in your dashboard represents the InstaPay Plus amount you can withdraw for that day.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/2-how-is-the-instapay-plus-amount-calculated/

### What is InstaPay Plus Payment?

InstaPay Plus Payment is a feature that allows merchants to receive a portion of their expected Cash on Delivery (COD) collections based on the “Sorted” status visible in the merchant dashboard.
This helps improve cash flow and ensures you have working capital available when needed.

Source: https://help.pathao.com/1-what-is-instapay-plus-payment/
